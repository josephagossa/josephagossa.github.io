ML Models — KenyaESG-RoBERTa

Three independent binary sentence classifiers (one per ESG pillar) for Environmental, Social, and Governance communication in corporate reports of firms listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE). Fine-tuned roberta-base models, following the sentence-level design of Schimanski et al. (2024). Released under Apache-2.0.

Dataset — KenyaESG

KenyaESG: A Sentence-level ESG Disclosure Classification Dataset for Nairobi Securities Exchange Corporate Reports.

A sentence-level dataset for three independent binary ESG classification tasks (Environmental, Social, Governance), built from the annual, integrated, and sustainability reports of firms listed on the Nairobi Securities Exchange (NSE), 2010–2024, combined with reference sentences from Schimanski et al. (2024). For each pillar it provides a fine-tuning set of 3,900 sentences and a disjoint held-out human evaluation set of 100 sentences with two independent annotators' judgments (Cohen's κ: 0.94 E, 0.86 S, 0.88 G). Training labels for the Kenya-sourced sentences were assigned by a keyword filter refined by a single-reviewer pass; the human-annotated evaluation sets are the appropriate performance benchmark. The dataset captures disclosure intensity rather than quality. Company and personal names are masked ([COMPANY], [PERSON]); masking is semi-automatic and not guaranteed exhaustive, and the data must not be used to re-identify individuals or firms. Companion data to the KenyaESG-RoBERTa classifiers. Licensed under Apache-2.0.
