Research

My research interests include Econometrics, Big Data, Machine Learning / Text as Data (NLP), Large Language Models (LLMs), Generative AI, ESG disclosure, corporate governance, and health economics. I work at the intersection of finance, development economics, and machine learning, and also contribute statistical methodology to interdisciplinary work in health sciences.

Peer-Reviewed Publications

CADA-PRO: A Patient Questionnaire Measuring Key Cognitive, Motor, Emotional, and Behavioral Outcomes in CADASIL
C. Di Folco, A. Jabouley, S. Reyes, C. Machado, S. Guey, D. Hervé, F. Fernandes, J. Agossa, H. Chabriat, S. Tezenas du Montcel

Stroke, 2024;55:2439–2448 (American Heart Association). Development and validation of the first patient-reported outcome questionnaire (CADA-PRO) for cerebral small vessel disease, including CADASIL, covering cognitive, motor, emotional, and behavioral domains. Contributed to the statistical methodology and psychometric analysis (factor analysis, item response theory, reliability and concurrent-validity testing) on a two-stage study of 133 patients.

Published · DOI: 10.1161/STROKEAHA.124.047692
Quantitative Analysis of Cell Aggregation Dynamics Identifies HDAC Inhibitors as Potential Regulators of Cancer Cell Clustering
F. Gava, J. Pignolet, S. Déjean, O. Mondésert, R. Morin, J. Agossa, B. Ducommun, V. Lobjois

Cancers, 2021;13:5840 (MDPI). Quantitative study of anchorage-independent aggregation across 25 cancer cell lines, linking aggregation dynamics to gene expression. Performed the analysis of the transcriptional data, using sparse PLS / LASSO variable selection (R, mixOmics) on CCLE expression data and the Connectivity Map resource to identify genes and perturbagens (HDAC inhibitors) associated with cell clustering.

Published · DOI: 10.3390/cancers13225840

Joseph Agossa (sole author)
